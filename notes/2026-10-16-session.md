## Data file format
Columns: `id|label|year|season|track|fixed_date|first_reading|psalm|second_reading|gospel|notes|source_url`

- `id`: `<year>-<slug>[-t1|-t2]`, e.g. `a-advent-1`, `b-proper-11-t2`, `abc-st-andrew`.
- `year`: `A`, `B`, `C`, or `ABC` (same readings every year).
- `season`: advent, christmas, epiphany, lent, holy_week, easter (incl. Ascension, Pentecost), after_pentecost, holy_day.
- `track`: `1` (semicontinuous) / `2` (related) for Propers 4–29; blank otherwise.
- `fixed_date`: `MM-DD` for fixed-date days; blank for movable days.
- Citation syntax is specified in `notes/citation-format.md` (` or ` = alternatives, `, ` = multiple
  selections, chapters always explicit, parentheses = optional verses).
- Palm Sunday `gospel` is the Passion; the Liturgy of the Palms is in `notes`.

## Source and decisions
- Source: lectionarypage.net (Episcopal RCL), one page per day; URL kept in `source_url`.
- Excluded: Easter Vigil, Easter Day early/evening services, weekdays of Lent/Easter Week, Propers 1–2
  (they never fall after Trinity Sunday; same as Epiphany 6–7).
- Editorial fixes (flagged in `notes`): A Proper 11 and B Proper 20 — the Wisdom of Solomon alternative is
  printed on the Track 1 line of the source page but placed with Track 2 per the RCL pattern; B Proper 8
  Track 2 follows the source page's own editor's note (Lamentations 3:21-33 as an alternative first reading).
- Episcopal-specific readings preserved as given (e.g. C Lent 3 = Exodus 3:1-15; Christmas 1 uses John 1:1-18 every year).

## Book URN table (same session)
- `data/book_urns.txt` (`book|urn`): maps each book name cited in `rcl_readings.txt` to its
  book-level CTS URN in the compnov corpus, *without* a version identifier
  (`Genesis|urn:cts:compnov:bible.genesis:`). Add a version with `rcl.books.versioned_urn`.
  Sources: `corpus/compnov.cex` (OT/apocrypha: masoretic, onkelos, septuagint, vulgate) and
  `corpus/compnov_nt.cex` (NT: greeknt, vulgate) in github.com/neelsmith/compnov.
- 62 books, 62 URNs (Daniel and Esther have two rows each: `daniel`/`daniel2`, `esther`/`esther2`;

- Name differences: Psalm -> psalms, Song of Solomon -> songs, Wisdom of Solomon -> wisdom,
  Romans -> rom, Revelation -> revelations; Septuagint Daniel and Esther are the works
  `daniel2` and `esther2`.
- Gaps: **Luke and John (the Gospels) are not in either corpus file**; they have one row with an
  empty URN. Nehemiah has no Septuagint; Baruch, Judith, Sirach, Wisdom have no Masoretic.
- Caution for later passage lookup: versions number differently (e.g. Septuagint/Vulgate
  Psalm 22 = BCP/Hebrew Psalm 23; Hebrew verse numbers often count psalm titles).
